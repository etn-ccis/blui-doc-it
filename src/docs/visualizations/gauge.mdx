# Gauge charts

Gauge charts display a single qualitative value within a defined range. By mapping a measurement onto an arc, they let users quickly assess the status or health of a metric at a glance.

### When to use

Gauge charts work well for satisfying the following user needs, among others:

- Communicating a single key performance indicator against a target or range;

- Showing system health, quality scores, or progress toward a goal;

- Highlighting whether a value falls within a healthy, warning, or critical threshold.

## Best practices

<p style={{fontSize: '18px',fontWeight: '600',}}>Use thresholds to communicate status</p>

Pairing the value with color-coded threshold zones lets users instantly judge whether a measurement is healthy or at risk, without having to interpret the number on its own. Keep the threshold colors consistent with their meaning across your dashboards.

<ImageGrid
    images={[GaugeBpThreshold, GaugeBpValue]}
    captionsUnderImages={[
        'DO:Color-coded zones let users instantly judge whether a value is healthy or at risk',
        'DONT:A bare value with no thresholds forces users to interpret the number on its own'
    ]}
/>

<p style={{fontSize: '18px',fontWeight: '600',}}>Keep the gauge undistorted</p>

Move supporting detail, such as the definition of each threshold, into a tooltip triggered by an info mark instead of crowding the face of the gauge. Always preserve the circular arc; stretching or squeezing it distorts the proportions and misrepresents the value.

<ImageGrid
    images={[GaugeBpInfomark, GaugeBpStretch]}
    captionsUnderImages={[
        'DO:Keep a clean circular arc and reveal extra detail in a tooltip',
        'DONT:Stretching or squeezing the gauge breaks the arc and misrepresents the value'
    ]}
/>

<p style={{fontSize: '18px',fontWeight: '600',}}>Keep the scale simple</p>

A gauge is most effective when it communicates one value at a glance. Use a clear minimum and maximum, label only the values that matter (such as threshold boundaries), and avoid packing multiple metrics into a single gauge.
