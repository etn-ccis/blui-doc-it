## Heatmap

A heatmap maps values to a 2D grid through color intensity, giving an at-a-glance view of how data distributes across two dimensions. It helps users quickly spot high/low regions, recurring patterns, and outliers.

### Chart behaviour

<p style={{fontSize: '18px',fontWeight: '600',}}>Entry motion</p>

The cell grid is progressively revealed column by column from left to right while fading in, conveying a sense of data gradually emerging.

<p style={{fontSize: '18px',fontWeight: '600',}}>Hover state</p>

Highlights the active link and its connected nodes while de-emphasizing the rest, making a single flow easy to trace.

## Line Bar Composed Chart

Combines bars and lines within one coordinate system to compare metrics of different scales or types. It supports multiple Y-axes to carry several data series with different units at once, flexibly adapting to multi-dimensional analysis.

## Chart behaviour

<p style={{fontSize: '18px',fontWeight: '600',}}>Entry motion</p>

Bars grow from the baseline, and lines draw from left to right, continuing the separate effects of bars and lines.

<p style={{fontSize: '18px',fontWeight: '600',}}>Hover state</p>

Simultaneously highlights the bar and point(s) at the same X position and displays their combined values in a tooltip.
